Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Data Collection and Usage

This application ("Cebim Muhasebe") stores income, expense, recurring bills, investment portfolio, and credit card records entered by users strictly on your device's local storage. None of your financial records are tracked, monitored, or transmitted to any remote servers operated by the developer or third parties.

2. Optional Google Sheets & Local Backup

Users may voluntarily configure cloud backup via their personal Google Sheets account. When configured, data transmissions happen directly and encrypted (HTTPS) between your mobile device and your own Google Apps Script webhook. No developer or intermediary server receives or accesses this data. Users can also export/import full JSON backup files locally at any time.

3. Permissions

The application may request local notification permissions solely to provide reminders for credit card payment deadlines, billing cycles, or scheduled recurring payments. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device without external push servers.

4. Data Security & Deletion

Because your data is stored exclusively on your device, you have complete authority over it. You can permanently delete all records at any time directly through the application's "Reset All Data" option in settings or by clearing the application storage via your device settings.

This privacy policy may be updated periodically to reflect new features. The latest version will always remain accessible inside the app and at this address.
